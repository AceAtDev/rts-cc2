# Tank turns before translating

The [pinned Liberty SiegeTank catalog](https://github.com/Talv/sc2-data/blob/1921f856b0443d4cbd366c472cd7984fa6a224d1/mods/liberty.sc2mod/base.sc2data/GameData/UnitData.xml) enables `TurnBeforeMove=1` and sets `TurningRate=360`. It does not override [Core's inherited `StationaryTurningRate=720`](https://github.com/Talv/sc2-data/blob/1921f856b0443d4cbd366c472cd7984fa6a224d1/mods/core.sc2mod/base.sc2data/GameData/UnitData.xml). These are distinct catalog fields; stationary turning is not an arbitrary doubled movement rate.

The previously omitted flag allowed a Tank to translate toward a new destination while facing away. Its profile now includes the flag and stationary rate. When the body needs to align with its next movement leg, the executor stops translation and turns at the stationary rate. It can translate on the update that finishes alignment. Existing moving-turn rate remains available for velocity steering, and infantry keeps its immediate translational response.

Three SC2 4.10.0.75689 fixtures prepared a stationary Tank facing east, north or west before issuing a ten-game-unit eastward Move on Flat64. Native observations show first translation on relative loops 1, 2 and 4 respectively, with empty orders on loops 73, 74 and 76. Translation is then 0.140625 game units per loop until the exact endpoint clamp. The prototype now matches every relative position and order lifetime in those three recordings.

Native stationary heading increments are approximately 0.785156 radians per loop. Our continuous catalog conversion yields π/4 per loop at Faster. Across these three stationary captures, facing differs by less than 0.001 radians. This comparison establishes movement start and completion timing; it does not establish bit-exact native orientation quantization or all turning behavior under crowd avoidance, ramps or moving target pursuit.

A further native fixture reversed an already-moving Tank after ten eastward loops. It stopped translation for loops 11–13 and resumed westward at loop 14, turning at the stationary rate. The same stop interval and every relative position/order lifetime match after the correction. At a 180-degree reversal, the continuous model's tie direction can differ from the native quantized orientation; this is not claimed as matching facing for that fixture.

`node tests/tank-turn-fidelity.mjs` runs seven checks for catalog profile fields, the three stationary start timings, translation on the alignment update, a new perpendicular waypoint, combat target preservation, unchanged infantry responsiveness and moving reversal timing. Optional `--trace-dir /absolute/path/to/traces-tank-heading` compares every captured relative position/order lifetime and bounds the heading difference. Optional `--reversal-trace /absolute/path/to/tank-moving-reversal.jsonl` compares the reversal trajectory and order lifetime without claiming its facing matches. Native raw captures remain local.
