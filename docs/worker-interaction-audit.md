# Small worker interaction differences

Audit date: 2026-10-09. The six implementation gaps recorded in the earlier audit are now resolved in the supported Terran subset. Follow-up native measurements use historical SC2 **4.10.0.75689**, Faster speed and two Participant clients. Current construction changes are sourced separately from Blizzard's 5.0.14/5.0.16 notes. Implemented transitions, measured behavior and authored geometry are distinguished below; this is not a full-control-parity claim.

| Resolved gap | Implemented behavior | Evidence and remaining limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Command Center transport | Five passenger slots with approach reservations; visible workers board at contact, retain cargo/supply/group memory, and leave live selection. Actual passenger buttons unload individuals. Grounded destruction ejects living passengers; flying destruction kills them. | [Transport comparison](transport-fidelity.md), [module](../dist/transport.js). Catalog search8/range1 and native 4.10 comparisons support contact-based loading. Grounded Load/Unload, including Shift, execute immediately during Train. Shift flying Load/Unload wait behind an active Move; ordinary flying Load preserves that Move, while ordinary flying Unload ejects immediately and cancels it. Generic queueable-command guidance did not establish a ground production barrier. Exact approach braking, contact and multiple-passenger exits remain authored. |
| Construction-worker movement | Initial approach retains ordinary collisions. Working SCVs move within their current structure footprint with body collision disabled while progress continues; other obstacles still block service legs. Halt, replacement and death release ownership. Additional queued builders wait with their successor orders intact and can take over. | [Construction comparison](construction-fidelity.md), [module](../dist/construction.js). Native 4.10 supports footprint passage with progress on every moving loop. The 4.64–6.07 real-second relocation bounds come from [5.0.14](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24162754/starcraft-ii-5-0-14-patch-notes); occupied sequential Build acceptance and melee Attack retention follow [5.0.16](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24259080/starcraft-ii-5-0-16-patch-notes). Service-point selection, RNG, steering and waiting/takeover geometry are prototype policies, not modern native captures. |
| Gas successors and repeated commands | One hidden extractor serves each Refinery. A queued Move promotes at emergence with four gas, before deposit. Repeated Gather/Smart during visible approach preserves arrival/extraction. Gather with existing cargo visits the selected resource first, then returns or promotes its successor at contact. | [Gas/cargo observations](native-worker-gas-followup.md), [worker module](../dist/workers.js). The single-worker fixture hides at loop 23, emerges 55 and deposits 81; its Move successor starts 55. These are historical fixture-specific loops. Commands to a known hidden-worker tag returned Error/NotSupported through the native API; the host rejects inaccessible input, without inferring an extraction reset or universal native UI behavior. |
| Repair contexts and starvation | Idle/Patrol autocast can approach and restore a return Move/Patrol; Hold repairs only at contact without pursuit. Move suppresses acquisition. Unfunded manual Repair is rejected; unfunded Smart falls back to Follow. Exhaustion promotes the saved context or successor, with no implicit manual restart when funds return. | [Repair context comparison](repair-autocast-fidelity.md), [intent module](../dist/repair-autocast.js). Native 4.10 comparisons distinguish these contexts and funded acquisition. Nearest-candidate ordering and contact tolerance remain authored. Final-tick restoration/payment already use actual missing HP through [repair quotes](repair-cost-fidelity.md); native fractional resource rounding remains unmeasured. |
| Rally target loss and feedback | Selected owner producers display complete world/minimap chains with live friendly endpoints. Known-lost ordinary unit heads are removed without losing valid tails. Lost mineral heads remain Gather-at-point; workers reacquire locally or complete travel to an empty point before promoting the tail. Combat births convert every resource link to Move. | [Queue/rally comparison](order-queue-rally-followup.md), [gas/resource-point observations](native-worker-gas-followup.md), [queue module](../dist/order-queues.js). Catalog ClearRallyOnTargetLost defaults and the MineralField exception agree with native 4.10 produced-worker observations. Local polling intervals/search weights remain prototype policies; a distant expansion cannot silently replace the saved point. |
| Unit and rally queue bounds | Admission limits unit orders to 32 total including the active order, and rally targets to 4 including the head. Automatic Repair's saved return counts as a successor. Overflow cannot charge for a new placement, refund an existing plan or produce a false success marker; normal replacement remains available. | [Queue/rally comparison](order-queue-rally-followup.md), [queue module](../dist/order-queues.js). Native 4.10 Marine trials retain 32 after 33/100 submissions, while produced SCVs retain 4 rally orders after 5/6/100 targets. API Success on overflow and the producer's single raw rally target were insufficient evidence by themselves. The four-target rule is applied to the supported producer subset; modern-build/producer differences remain unmeasured. Production's five slots are separate. |

Existing explicit mineral Gather versus Smart, cargo-wait deferral, no-home cargo continuation and destroyed-field recovery retain their earlier native comparisons; see [worker recovery](worker-recovery.md). The carried-Gather deposit-first expectation in older tests has been corrected to the measured resource-first behavior. Started-building cancellation uses the pinned `.75` refund factor. Queued placement payment moments, shared planned-building handoff and cancellation recovery still need native comparison; passing prototype integration checks do not establish native payment timing.

The follow-up passes 49 [remaining-details browser checks](../tests/remaining_details_integration.py) and 26 [construction browser checks](../tests/construction_integration.py). These cover actual dispatch/hotkeys, transport during Train and flight, passenger UI, gas emergence/hidden input, repair context restoration, queue rejection, rally recovery and construction ownership. See [the integration report](remaining-controls-integration.md) for isolated suites and regression coverage. The tests verify implemented behavior; they do not certify human-perceived smoothness or exact native trajectories.

The pinned catalog and available native research client are historical. Modern patch notes cannot retroactively make the older capture evidence for current behavior. Only derived observations are published; licensed executables, maps, assets and raw captures remain private, outside the repository. Exact collision steering, construction RNG, contact/exit geometry, repair rounding and complete current-client parity remain open fidelity work.
