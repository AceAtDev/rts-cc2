# Construction worker service movement

The pinned [TerranBuild ability](https://github.com/Talv/sc2-data/blob/1921f856b0443d4cbd366c472cd7984fa6a224d1/mods/liberty.sc2mod/base.sc2data/GameData/AbilData.xml) enables `PeonDisableCollision` and selects the `Construction` mover. The pinned [MoverData](https://github.com/Talv/sc2-data/blob/1921f856b0443d4cbd366c472cd7984fa6a224d1/mods/liberty.sc2mod/base.sc2data/GameData/MoverData.xml) defines that mover as Ground with `PathMode=Builder`.

The older catalog specifies Normal-speed fidget delays of 5–10 seconds. Blizzard's [5.0.14 patch notes](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24162754/starcraft-ii-5-0-14-patch-notes) narrow the real-time delay between SCV movements while building from 3.57–7.14 to **4.64–6.07 seconds**. This helper uses the newer documented bounds. Its per-worker/building RNG, wait starting after arrival, perimeter selection, and steering are explicit prototype policies; they do not claim the exact native distribution, trajectories, or phase timing.

`createConstruction({move, clear, clock, seed=0})` exposes `update(worker, dt)` and `release(worker)`. Update returns `approach`, `working`, `waiting`, `complete`, or `invalid`. The ordinary movement module handles initial contact with normal collisions. On contact, the working state periodically selects a reachable service point and moves using the worker's speed and acceleration. Service legs ignore the current building footprint through `clear(from, to, radius, building)`; other buildings, terrain, and world boundaries still block them. A newly blocked leg is abandoned without teleporting. Root integration owns construction progress, placement activation, completion notifications, and queue promotion; progress can continue for every `working` tick, including relocation.

`activeConstructionTarget(worker)` checks exact order identity, a live worker, a live unfinished target, and builder ownership. Body collisions are suppressed only during this working phase. Accepted replacement commands, completion, target/worker death, and loading invalidate the predicate immediately. `release` clears service state and unfinished builder ownership without changing queued orders. The game must release on command replacement, Stop, completion, reset, and death, and exempt the active worker from its ordinary building-overlap ejection pass.

Blizzard's [5.0.16 notes](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24259080/starcraft-ii-5-0-16-patch-notes) fix melee attackers losing their Attack order when a construction SCV moves through a structure. This implementation leaves explicit attack identity intact; ordinary combat cannot reach a blocked worker through the building but continues following it when it becomes reachable again.

The same patch fixes SCVs being unable to queue sequential Build orders onto structures already being constructed by another SCV. Such orders now remain active in a `waiting` phase with their successors intact. The waiting worker approaches ordinary exterior contact with collision enabled; only the existing builder advances progress. Completion promotes the successor, while Halt or death of the existing builder permits takeover. Queue acceptance is primary-source supported; this waiting/contact/takeover execution policy is conservative prototype behavior and has not been measured in the modern native engine. The available 4.10 client predates the documented queue fix.

A private historical 4.10 Depot capture supports interior construction movement with continuous progress: first progress at loop 27, motion segments 28–53, 151–168, 273–291, 405–419, and completion 506. Progress increased on every observed movement loop, about 1/480 per loop. SCV center distance from building center ranged about 0.2821–1.1445 game units during construction. These derived observations support footprint passage and continuing progress; one old-client trial does not establish the modern relocation distribution or exact prototype trajectories. No raw captures or assets are included here.

`node tests/construction_fidelity.mjs` checks travel/service collision phases, visible relocation, own-footprint passage, reproducibility, obstacle changes, ownership/resumption, cancellation, completion/death/loading, queue preservation, and explicit melee target persistence. Exact native construction contact geometry remains a separate measurement task, especially because the available historical native client predates these modern patch changes.

`python tests/construction_integration.py` checks the production placement and command dispatcher: contact and relocation with ongoing progress, completion into Shift-queued mining, Halt while inside the footprint with bounded exit and Smart resumption, planned/started cancellation refunds, dead-builder replacement, occupied sequential Build queues with ordinary waiting collision and single-owner progress, takeover after Halt/death, and an enemy melee Attack surviving footprint relocation and dealing damage again after the worker exits. The capture is `artifacts/construction-integration.png`.
